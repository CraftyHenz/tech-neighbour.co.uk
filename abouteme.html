How I work

The things I'll never compromise on.

01

Plain English, always

No jargon. No making you feel like you should already know this. I explain everything clearly and I'm happy to go over it twice.

02

Honest advice

If your laptop isn't worth repairing, I'll tell you. If there's a free solution, I'll point you to it. I'd rather keep your trust than make a quick sale.

03

No fix, no fee

If I can't solve the problem, you don't pay. It's that straightforward. I won't charge you for time spent on something that didn't work.
